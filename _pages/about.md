I lead data science at Info Edge for Naukri and Jeevansathi, and I am pursuing a PhD in Graph Machine Learning at IIT Delhi. I also write [Network Effect Labs]({{ site.data.writing.publication.url }}), essays on how network effects shape businesses.

#### Research

My PhD, under the guidance of [Prof. Srikanta Bedathur](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~srikanta/), focuses on temporal graph representation learning and generative modeling, with papers at AAAI, ICML, SIGIR, TMLR and LoG. [Publications →]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})

#### Industry

I own data science strategy and execution across two-sided marketplaces: conversational AI and LLMs, CV–job matching and recommendation, trust & safety, pricing and engagement. [Experience →]({{ '/experience/' | relative_url }})

#### Writing

[Network Effect Labs]({{ site.data.writing.publication.url }}) is a series of essays on network effects in business: what they are, where they matter, and how matching, pricing and acquisition decide whether a platform compounds. [All writing →]({{ '/writing/' | relative_url }})

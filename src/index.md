I lead the Centre of Excellence at Info Edge that builds conversational agents on in-house infrastructure for B2C and B2B products, including the jobseeker conversational agent, the recruiter agent and the 99acres agent.

Earlier, I worked extensively on recommendation and search for [Naukri.com](https://www.naukri.com/), delivering 100–400% jumps in value across various models. I then built the Jeevansathi data science team from scratch and grew it from 1 to 5 people. Its systems included two-way matching that improved acceptance by 100%, a revenue system that raised revenue directly attributable to data science by approximately 15–18%, and spam detection that brought the time to identify a spammer under 2 hours.

In parallel, I was fortunate to do my PhD under the guidance of [Prof. Srikanta Bedathur](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~srikanta/), who took me on as his student. With him I built my understanding of graphs, graph ML and temporal graph modeling, which led to [publications](/publications/) at A* venues. He taught me the fundamentals of research, first-principles thinking and the virtue of perseverance.

I also write [Network Effect Labs]({{ writing.publication.url }}), essays on how network effects shape businesses.
